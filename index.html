About

A family retreat in the old capital of Bengal

Milton's Garden is a private family farm house in Vottopur, Sonargaon, beside the Sonargaon ISKCON Temple. The property is set among fruit trees and vegetable gardens, with a fishing pond, an open lawn with a barbecue area, and a comfortable house with rooms and a kitchen.

It is a peaceful country retreat from Dhaka for the family and their invited guests.
